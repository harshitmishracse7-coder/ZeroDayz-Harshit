University overview • Demo summary

About Galgotias University

Galgotias University is located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, and is sponsored by the Smt. Shakuntala Educational and Welfare Society. The university’s public overview describes its focus on multidisciplinary education, research, innovation, and student development.

2011–12Academic session in which the university began operations
NAAC A+Accreditation stated on the university overview page
30K+Students, as listed in the university overview
200+Programmes across diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and PhD levels

Facts summarized from the official university overview and vision page. Information can change; refer to the university for current details.
